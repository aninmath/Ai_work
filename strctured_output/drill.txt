Depot Name: BNG-ECO, South Depot
Date: 03 October 2025
Time of Visit: 11:00 AM
Duration: 1 hour 15 minutes
Conducted By: Regional Safety Compliance Authority
Visit Type: Scheduled Regulatory Inspection

Objectives:

To verify compliance with fire safety and emergency response protocols.
To inspect critical infrastructure including hydrant systems and monitoring equipment.
To review documentation and staff preparedness.


Summary of Events:
At 11:00 AM, the inspection team arrived at the depot and initiated the regulatory visit. The team was escorted by the Safety & Compliance Officer and began with a walkthrough of the facility.

Hydrant System: Pressure and flow tests were conducted on all hydrants. All units passed the inspection.
Monitoring Equipment: Fire alarm panels and smoke detectors were tested for responsiveness and calibration. No faults were detected.
Documentation Review: Safety logs, drill records, and equipment maintenance sheets were reviewed and found to be up-to-date.


Observations:

Hydrant system is well-maintained and operational.
Monitoring systems are responsive and correctly configured.
Staff demonstrated awareness of emergency protocols.
Safety documentation is properly maintained.


Issues Noted:

One hydrant valve showed signs of corrosion and was flagged for preventive maintenance.
Emergency lighting in the east wing corridor was dim and needs replacement.


Recommendations:

Schedule preventive maintenance for the corroded hydrant valve.
Replace emergency lighting units in the east wing corridor.
Continue regular internal audits to ensure ongoing compliance.


Conclusion:
The regulatory visit was successfully completed with positive feedback from the inspection team. Minor issues were identified and corrective actions have been initiated. The depot remains in compliance with regional safety standards.
